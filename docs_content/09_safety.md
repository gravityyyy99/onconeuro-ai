# Safety, ethics and honesty

*Why this website keeps telling you not to use it for health decisions.*

## The short version

**OncoNeuro AI is a learning project, not a medical tool.** It cannot diagnose anyone. If you have a health concern, see a qualified doctor.

## The longer version

**It can be wrong in both directions.** A false "no tumor" could make someone feel safe when they should not. A false "tumor" could cause terrible fear. Both harms are real.

**It can sound sure while wrong.** The probabilities are not honest odds. A 95% answer can still be a mistake.

**It does not know what it does not know.** Show it something unlike its training data and it still picks one of four answers.

**It has not been tested the way medicine requires.** Real medical software is studied in clinical trials, checked for bias across ages, sexes and populations, and approved by regulators. This project has done none of that.

**Medicine is more than a picture.** A doctor combines the full scan with your story, your symptoms, your exam and other tests.

## What we did about it

- A welcome message every visit, in plain words.
- A reminder on the main page and in the menu.
- Warnings next to results, stronger when the model is unsure.
- Results published with the bad numbers included, especially the missed gliomas.
- No storing of uploaded pictures.

## What you should do

- Treat everything here as a classroom demonstration.
- Never upload a picture to decide about your health or anyone else's.
- If you are worried about your health, speak to a doctor.
- If you are a student, use this project to learn how such tools work and why they need careful testing.
